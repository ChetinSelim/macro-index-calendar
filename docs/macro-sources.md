## Sources and time evidence

- FOMC dates: <https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm>.
  The event is the policy decision on the final meeting day, not both meeting
  days. Cross-month meetings use the final month. The prose note currently
  publishes the 25–26 January 2028 meeting. The Fed states that each meeting date
  is tentative until confirmed at the preceding meeting; future events are
  conservatively marked provisional. The 14:00 Eastern time policy is official:
  <https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20130313a.htm>.
- Bank of England dates:
  <https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy/upcoming-mpc-dates>.
  The published 2026 and 2027 tables explicitly say confirmed. Noon publication
  of the decision and minutes is stated at
  <https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy>. Events use Europe/London.
- BLS live source: <https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/bls.ics>.
  Normal fallback HTML sources are
  <https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/empsit.htm> and
  <https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/cpi.htm>.
  Direct requests to all three returned HTTP 403 on the verification run. The
  official HTML tables were separately readable through web retrieval and
  reviewed to make `data/macro-bls-reviewed.json`, including the reporting months
  and 08:30 release times. The official annual calendar explicitly states that
  all calendar times are Eastern:
  <https://www.bls.gov/schedule/2027/home.htm>.
- BEA live ICS:
  <https://www.bea.gov/news/schedule/ics/online-calendar-subscription.ics>.
  This carries explicit UTC timestamps (08:30 America/New_York after conversion)
  and Personal Income and Outlays titles containing the reporting period. PCE
  price indexes are part of that release. The official HTML fallback reads both
  <https://www.bea.gov/news/schedule> and
  <https://www.bea.gov/news/schedule/next-year>.

## Identity, degradation, and maintenance

Release identities use the BLS/BEA reporting period, not release day or a
provider UID that may encode the day. Combined BEA reporting months are
supported, including its historic October and November 2025 joint release.
Central-bank identities use the scheduled meeting's ordinal in its year, so a
meeting-date change preserves its identity. Inserting/deleting an additional
regular meeting would require identity review; unscheduled FOMC notation votes
are excluded from the regular decision schedule.

BLS attempts ICS first and each category's HTML independently. On access or
parse failure, the reviewed seed is returned with `status=partial`, its fixed
`verified_at=2026-11-02T18:32:34Z`, and an explicit note that it is not freshly
network verified. The stamp never advances on a failed fetch. After 90 days it
returns unavailable and requires another manual review of official schedules.
The coordinator may retain prior good events, but must keep their degraded
health visible. No request headers, cookies, proxies, or browser session were
used to work around the block. New schedule dates are never generated by a
monthly release rule.

Unrecognized layout/date/reporting-period fields raise an error instead of
silently deleting the category. A failed BEA ICS fetch attempts both official
HTML schedules and raises if either required schedule cannot be parsed. Runtime
failure details are left visible to the coordinator.

## Verification

`python -m pytest tests/test_macro_sources.py -q` passes 10 tests. Tests cover
extracted official Fed/BOE/BEA fixtures, exact decision/release dates, source
agreement between BEA ICS and HTML, DST conversion, identities after a schedule
change, cross-month FOMC dates, combined PCE periods, blocked BLS degradation,
independent HTML category recovery, and seed expiry. BLS HTML/ICS parser tests
use compact structural fixtures because runtime access was blocked; the
reviewed fallback itself is based on the official tables cited above.

Official fixture subsets under `data/macro-fixtures` are captured source
content for tests, not runtime fallback schedules.
